# Locations & Industries Keyword Map
**Source:** Semrush US database, live search volume/CPC/KD, pulled 2026-08-11
**Scope:** State pages, city pages, and industry pages on acendia.us
**Prepared by:** Head of SEO pass, following the same methodology as `acendia-keyword-briefs.md` (services)

---

## Method

For each page, the primary keyword is the highest-relevance, real-search-volume phrase available in Semrush for that geography/industry. Where the "obvious" phrase had zero indexed volume (no data), a validated alternate was substituted — this happened for two states (see flagged rows). Intent codes: 0 = commercial, 1 = informational, 2 = transactional.

1. **`/industries/law-firm-seo/`** — 9,900 vol, $66.20 CPC, KD 31. The single highest-value page in this map by a wide margin. Already well-optimized from Phase 2; recommend this be the first page to receive a backlink/authority-building push.

## Changes Made This Pass

1. **`/locations/illinois/`**: primary keyword switched from "seo agency illinois" (zero Semrush data) to "seo company illinois" (70 vol, KD 24). Title trimmed to `SEO Company Illinois`.
2. **`/locations/tennessee/`**: same issue — switched to "seo company tennessee" (480 vol, KD 9). Title trimmed to `SEO Company Tennessee`.
3. **`/industries/automotive/`**: title/meta/hero rewritten to include the literal, high-opportunity phrase "automotive SEO" (KD 17 — the best ratio in this entire dataset), replacing a title that only implied the topic.
4. **`/industries/home-services/`**: title trimmed and tightened to lead with "Home Services SEO," matching the literal 2,400-volume phrase.
5. **Title-length cleanup across all 56 pages**: every state (20), city (15), and industry (21) metaTitle previously ran 60–92 characters once the brand suffix was appended — well over the ~60-character guidance already applied to the service pages. All were trimmed to their core keyword phrase (e.g. `SEO Agency Texas | Digital Marketing for Texas Businesses` → `SEO Agency Texas`; `SEO for Law Firms | Legal SEO Services` → `Law Firm SEO`, matching the real 9,900-volume term exactly). Every title is now under 50 characters including the brand suffix. Meta descriptions and H1s were left as-is except on the 4 pages where the primary keyword itself changed (Illinois, Tennessee, Automotive, Home Services) — the underlying page copy was already well-aligned with realistic search patterns from the Phase 2/3 build, so this was a title-tag-precision pass, not a copy rewrite.
